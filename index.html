    Kitna time lagta hai?

    10 minute ke video par aam taur pe 3-8 minute. Pehli baar thoda zyada, kyunki AI model download hota hai. Ek time par ek hi video process hota hai, baaki line mein lagte hain.

    Kisi ka bhi video clip kar sakta hu?

    Sirf apne videos ya jinki permission ho. Dusron ke videos upload karne par copyright strike aa sakta hai.

    "YouTube ne block kiya" likha aaye to?

    Kabhi kabhi YouTube server se download rok deta hai. Tab "Video upload" tab mein video file daalo, ya tool ko apne PC par chalao.

    Clips kab tak rahenge?

    Kuch ghante tak. Turant download kar lo.

    Free hai kya?

    Haan, poora free hai, koi login ya payment nahi.
